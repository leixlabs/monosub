Hey, I'm an indie developer passionate about creating software that puts users first — no subscriptions, no data harvesting, just honest tools that work.

**Why I built MonoSub:**

I was tired of paying monthly fees for simple tasks and uploading sensitive videos to the cloud just to get subtitles. There had to be a better way — something local, fast, and actually affordable.

So I built MonoSub: a one-time purchase subtitle generator that runs 100% on your Mac. Your videos never leave your computer. Pay once, own forever.

**My promise:**
- 🛠️ Active development & lifetime free updates
- 💬 Real human support (that's me!)
